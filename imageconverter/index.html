❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an image?
Tap the dashed square to pick images from your photo library, choose your target format, adjust quality or resize if needed, then tap Convert. Once done, you can save to Photos or share via any app.
What does the quality slider do?
For lossy formats (JPEG, WEBP, HEIC), the quality slider controls compression. Higher values = better visual quality but larger file size. PNG, TIFF, and BMP are lossless so the slider is hidden.
How does resize work?
Set a max width and/or height. The image scales down proportionally to fit within those bounds — aspect ratio is always preserved. Fill only one field and the other calculates automatically.
Can I convert multiple images at once?
Yes. Select multiple images and they'll all be converted with the same settings in one batch. Progress is shown for each image.
Where do converted images go?
They're saved temporarily in the app's storage. After conversion, tap "Save to Photos" to add them to your library, or "Share" to send them via any app.
What is "Preserve metadata"?
When enabled, EXIF data (camera model, date taken, location, etc.) is copied from the source image to the converted file. When disabled, the output is stripped clean.
Does the app collect my data?
No. Image Converter collects no personal data, no usage analytics, and no tracking information. All image processing is performed entirely on your device. No images are ever uploaded or transmitted.
Why is the icon black and white?
The app uses a clean black-and-white theme. No distractions — just the tools you need to convert images.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Image Converter respects your privacy. This policy describes how your information is handled when you use the app.

Data Collection
Image Converter does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All image processing is performed locally on your device. No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs are included in the app.

The app requests access to your photo library only to pick images for conversion and to save converted images back. Images are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted outside your device.

Image Converter does not access the internet. No network requests are made at any time. The app functions fully offline.

No third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that transmit data are used in this app. The only external dependencies are Apple's system frameworks (Image I/O, Core Graphics) and Expo's open-source modules.

Image Converter is safe for all ages. It does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
