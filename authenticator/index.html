🔐 About

Authenticator generates TOTP codes for 2FA entirely on-device. No accounts to create, no cloud sync, no servers. All secrets stay in local storage. Zero network permissions.

No analytics. No tracking. Works offline.

✨ Features

  • Generate TOTP 6-digit codes from standard secret keys
  • Save accounts with custom names
  • One-tap copy with haptic feedback
  • Real-time countdown showing code expiry
  • All data stored locally — fully offline
  • Zero network permissions, no analytics

❓ FAQ

How do I add an account?
Tap the Authenticator tab, enter a name, paste the secret key from your 2FA provider, then tap Save. The 6-digit code appears immediately.
Where's my secret key?
When enabling 2FA on a service (Google, GitHub, Instagram, X), look for Setup key or Secret key — a long string of letters and numbers.
How do I copy a code?
Tap the 6-digit code. It copies to your clipboard and a checkmark confirms it.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Authenticator never connects to the internet. Your secrets stay on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no analytics. Zero network permissions required.
Can I delete an account?
Yes. On the Accounts tab, tap the X icon next to any account. Confirm deletion when prompted.
