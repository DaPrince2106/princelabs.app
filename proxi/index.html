Proxi

VPN client — support & documentation

📖 About

Proxi creates a system-wide VPN tunnel on iOS that routes all device traffic through a SOCKS5 or HTTPS proxy you configure or purchase. Built on Apple's NEPacketTunnelProvider API for deep network integration.

No traffic logs. No data collection. No analytics. Everything runs on your device.

❓ FAQ

How do I connect?
Enter your proxy details (host, port, protocol) on the Proxy tab, then tap Connect. Proxi creates a VPN tunnel and routes all device traffic through your proxy. Use the built-in speed test to verify connectivity.
Having connection issues?
Verify your proxy server is running and reachable. Double-check host, port, and authentication credentials. Check the Logs tab for tunnel setup errors.
How do I buy proxies?
Open the Purchases tab. Select your provider, country, quantity, and period (7–90 days). Pay via cryptocurrency through NowPayments. Credentials are emailed after payment confirmation.
Is my data safe?
Absolutely. Proxi runs entirely on-device. No traffic logs, usage data, or personal information ever leaves your phone. Proxy credentials are stored locally and only if you choose to save them.

📋 Proxy Types

  • HTTPS (CONNECT) — with optional Basic authentication
