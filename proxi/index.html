📖 About Proxi

Proxi creates a system-wide VPN tunnel on iOS that routes all device traffic through a SOCKS5 or HTTPS proxy you configure or purchase. Built on Apple's NEPacketTunnelProvider API for low-level network integration.

No traffic logs, no data collection, no analytics. The VPN runs entirely on your device. Proxy credentials stay local.

❓ FAQ

How do I connect?
Enter proxy details (host, port, protocol) on the Proxy tab, then tap Connect. Proxi creates a VPN tunnel and routes all device traffic through your proxy. Test connectivity with the built-in speed test.
Connection issues?
Verify your proxy server is running and reachable. Double-check host, port, and credentials. Use the Logs tab to diagnose tunnel setup errors.
How do I buy proxies?
Open the Purchases tab. Select provider, country, quantity, and period (7–90 days). Pay via cryptocurrency through NowPayments. Credentials are emailed after confirmation.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Proxi runs entirely on-device. No traffic logs, usage data, or personal information ever leaves your phone. Proxy credentials are stored locally and only if you choose to save them.
